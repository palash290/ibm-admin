Steps to OPERATE your Mortgage Minimizer

Instructions

One week after making the annual premium payment into your policy(s), take the maximum available policy loan(s) and use that money to make an early payment on your mortgage principal.

Dollar for dollar that amount will become available in the HELOC. Withdraw that amount and use it to pay off your other debts as directed below.

Note, you are not making any additional payments, only changing who receives them. You borrowed money from the insurance carrier who placed a lien on your policy. Every payment to your policy loan reduces the lien, and increases the amount immediately available to be used for another purpose. For each credit and loan item paid out, the payments you were making will now be made to the policy loan. For example, if you were making a monthly payment towards your credit card, now that it is paid off, that same payment must be paid towards the policy loan.

  • In year 1

    Completely pay off Credit Card with a balance of $9,000.00. Completely pay off Credit Card with a balance of $7,000.00. Reduce your Car Loan by $8,049.63. Your redirected monthly payment towards the policy loan is now $241.53

  • In year 2

    Completely pay off Car Loan with a balance of $256.69. Your redirected monthly payment towards the policy loan is now $1,365.28. The left-over $30,638.04 from your HELOC draw now also goes towards your policy loan. Once the policy loan balance reaches zero, the $1,365.28 you redirected towards your policy loan now just increases your monthly surplus budget to $3,996.61 and is paid towards the HELOC balance each month instead.

  • From year 3 to year 7

    Now that all credit and loan items are paid, put the full available policy loan annually towards the mortgage until it's paid in full. The policy loan amount put on the mortgage is immediately available in the HELOC, withdraw this amount from the HELOC and pay back the policy loan. Complete this step within a one week period.

  • In year 2

    Completely pay off Car Loan with a balance of $256.69. Your redirected monthly payment towards the policy loan is now $1,365.28. The left-over $30,638.04 from your HELOC draw now also goes towards your policy loan. Once the policy loan balance reaches zero, the $1,365.28 you redirected towards your policy loan now just increases your monthly surplus budget to $3,996.61 and is paid towards the HELOC balance each month instead.

  • In year 8

    Continue to fund the policy(s) until the policy loan available exceeds the HELOC debt. Once that occurs, take a policy loan to pay off the entire HELOC balance in full. Now, your annual premium will be paid from your income and then use the balance of your surplus dollars to pay the policy loan until it is paid in full.

  • In year 10

    If you have followed the plan as designed, your final policy loan payment will be made this year, and you will be 100% debt free. The next chapter in controlling your cash and expanding your personal financial system is now an option. It's time to look towards future generations.
